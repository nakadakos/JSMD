# JSMD – Just a Simple Mod Downloader

JSMD is a fast, minimal, and lightweight Fabric mod downloader and manager for Minecraft. It allows users to search, select, and install mods directly from Modrinth with a beautiful, clean interface. 

---

## Features

* **Mod Search & Download**: Search Fabric mods directly from Modrinth and install with one click.
* **Manage Installed Mods**: Automatically scans your mods folder to detect currently installed mods and their versions.
* **Smart Version Switching**: Easily switch Minecraft versions. JSMD will safely backup your current mods to a customizable folder, and automatically download the compatible versions for your new target Minecraft version!
* **Multi-threaded & Fast**: High performance, multi-threaded downloads and asynchronous UI loading.

---

## How it works

1. Open JSMD
2. **Download Mods Tab**:
   - Select your target Minecraft version.
   - Search for the mods you want.
   - Click **Download Selected**.
3. **Manage Installed Mods Tab**:
   - Click **Scan Mods Folder** to view currently installed mods.
   - Use **Switch Version** to instantly migrate all your mods to a different Minecraft update!

Mods will be installed and backed up automatically to your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
